Weight: mirrored means leader and partner step on opposite feet (normal when facing). Parallel means the same foot (shadow, cuddle).

How to use
  1. Pick a start position on the left of the combo line (most combos start in open, left-to-right).
  2. Add moves from What can follow. Only moves that start where you are are listed.
  3. Tap a station on the line to replace it, insert after it or remove it. A red break means two moves don't connect; tap it for ways to reconnect.
  4. The leader view shows the couple from above: you at the bottom, your partner above.
  5. Practice plays the combo on the beat. Random invents one; More has the share link.
  6. Explore on the Map, browse and rate moves in the Library, add your own under My moves.

Keys: ← → select · Del remove · Esc back to “what can follow” · Ctrl+Z undo
