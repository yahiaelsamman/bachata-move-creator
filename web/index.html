Bachata Move Creator

Positions are the nodes, moves are the dominoes. Click the last domino to see what can follow.

How to use
  1. Pick a start position (most combos start in open, left-to-right).
  2. Add moves from What can follow. Only moves that fit the current position are listed, like dominoes.
  3. Click any domino to replace it, insert after it or remove it. A red bridge marker means two dominoes don't connect; click it for ways to reconnect.
  4. The leader view shows each position from above: you at the bottom, her above.
  5. Practice plays the combo on the beat. Share link sends it to someone.
  6. Explore everything on the map and in the move library, and add your own moves under My moves.

Keys: ← → select · Del remove · Esc back to “next move” · Ctrl+Z undo
