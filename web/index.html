Bachata Move Creator

Positions are the nodes, moves are the dominoes: each move starts where the last one ended.

How to use
  1. Pick a start position (most combos start in open, left-to-right) and your level.
  2. Add moves under What can follow. Only moves that fit where you are are listed, like dominoes.
  3. Tap a domino to replace it, insert after it or remove it. A red bridge means two dominoes don't connect; tap it for ways to reconnect.
  4. The leader view shows each position from above: you at the bottom, the follower above.
  5. Practice plays the combo on the beat. Random invents one; Share link sends it.
  6. Explore on the Map, browse the Library, add your own under My moves.
More

Weight: mirrored means leader and follower step on opposite feet (normal when facing). Parallel means the same foot (shadow, cuddle).

Keys: ← → select · Del remove · Esc back to “what can follow” · Ctrl+Z undo
