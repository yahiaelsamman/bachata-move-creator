## Patterns (our reading, to be confirmed)

1. **Flat and clean over glowy.** Liked: flat colour, grids, clear shapes. Disliked: neon glow, spotlight gradients, gold sheen, overlapping inks.
2. **Mid-century and 70s-80s, not "old-world".** Liked: peach/teal/mustard pastels, jazz sleeves, Rams. Disliked: Western, pirate, Victorian plate, deco, occult.
3. **Readable at a glance.** Disliked: text that fights its colours, things they "don't understand". Liked: systems (metro map, grid of counts).
4. **A theme should be how the app really works, not a costume.** Liked: metro lines (the graph), counts grid (the 8 counts), tiles (positions), cards as a mechanic.
5. **Warm and human.** Malecón, Vinyl and Journal are warm; Brutalist is honest. Slick and generic is what they reject.

## Words that help (they already use these)

- References to real things: Dieter Rams, Pulp Fiction, jazz, "Mr. Sandman" (meaning to be confirmed), Magic: The Gathering Arena.
- Feeling words: overdone, rough, artsy, intuitive.
- Reasons about use: can't read it, don't understand it.

## Open questions

- What "Mr. Sandman" means here (song, show, or just the feeling).
- Light or dark base (most likes were light).
- Clean machine look vs a few handmade touches, which is the base.
- Should moves and positions feel like cards you collect and combine?
